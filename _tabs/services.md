# Practical IT support for growing organizations

Reliable technology should make it easier to run your business—not create more work. I help small and growing organizations improve their IT systems, solve infrastructure problems, and build practical processes that can scale with them.

## How I can help

### IT operations and infrastructure

Get dependable support for the systems your team relies on, from networks, servers, and cloud services to device management and day-to-day operations.

### Security and risk reduction

Identify gaps, strengthen core security practices, and create a realistic plan for protecting devices, accounts, and business data.

### Systems and process improvement

Turn informal or inconsistent IT work into clear, repeatable processes—including service management, change management, patching, and device lifecycle planning.

## A straightforward approach

Every engagement starts with a conversation about what is working, what is getting in the way, and what a good outcome looks like. From there, I can help assess the current environment, recommend practical next steps, and carry the work through implementation.

Support can be scoped as a focused project, an assessment and roadmap, or ongoing advisory help.
